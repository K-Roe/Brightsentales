Each prompt used the approved Living Room illustration for style reference and requested a new 16:9, full-bleed 2D cartoon background with open foreground space for the existing sprites, gentle colours, a sensory-friendly composition, and no characters, text, UI or 3D content. Specific location instructions were:

- **Max's bedroom:** A cosy child's bed and bedside furniture, lavender, teal, golden yellow and coral accents, gentle star motifs, warm daylight; do not copy the living-room sofa or layout.
- **Maxine's bedroom:** A distinct cheerful room with coral and teal quilt, books and playful star details; a broad open floor for Maxine to move and wave.
- **Kitchen:** Rounded cabinets, simple breakfast table or counter, window and broad central floor area where Max can use his phone.
- **Garden:** Green lawn, safe path or patio, flowering borders, rounded trees, soft sky and a hint of Max's house; clear ground for both Brightlings.
- **House front:** Friendly small home with rounded doorway, flowerpots, calm path and clear space for Max and Maxine to stand and wave goodbye.

**Artwork approval: PASSED.** The five original PNGs and protected Living Room remain unchanged. Narrator selection and full Episode 1 production acceptance remain pending.
